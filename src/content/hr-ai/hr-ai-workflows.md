The biggest productivity gain often comes after a useful prompt has been used more than once.

Instead of rebuilding the instruction each time, turn recurring work into a short sequence with a review point between steps.

## Hiring
A hiring workflow can move from job-description drafting to platform-specific postings, screening questions, interview questions and scorecards, panel briefing, finalist comparison, and candidate communication.

## Onboarding
A repeatable onboarding sequence can connect pre-boarding messages, manager preparation, a role-specific 30-60-90 plan, buddy support, and an early feedback survey.

## Performance reviews
Organize manager notes, draft the review, convert vague goals into measurable options, prepare coaching conversations, and keep calibration and final judgment with managers and HR.

## Employee queries and cases
Document intake factually, diagnose the type of issue, prepare neutral questions, use de-escalation or mediation structures where appropriate, and keep formal investigation and outcome steps inside the organization's process.

## Compliance and documentation
Templates can improve consistency, but they do not make AI-generated policy language legally sufficient. Use AI to structure drafts and change logs, then apply jurisdiction-specific review and the organization's approval process.

A workflow becomes more valuable when the prompt variables stay visible, outputs are reviewed before they feed the next step, and the team saves versions that actually work.
