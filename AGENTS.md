# Mercer Lane Press — project working instructions

This repository is the live website source for Mercer Lane Press. For all work in this repository, read:
- [Production playbook](docs/PRODUCTION_PLAYBOOK.md) — source checks, brand, editing, product QA and approval gates.
- [Video production standard](docs/VIDEO_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md) — premium book-promotion motion graphics, restrained audio and shot-by-shot inspection.
- [Marketing assets playbook](docs/MARKETING_ASSETS_PLAYBOOK.md) — carousels, editorial copy, distribution and measurement.
- [Book intake instructions](docs/ADD_BOOK_WITH_CODEX.md) — verified manuscript/cover metadata and website publishing.
- [Search and AI discovery measurement](docs/ai-discovery-measurement.md) — definitions and attribution caveats.

These are operational guidelines, not authority to publish, spend money or claim results.

## Required practical templates
- Before drafting or rendering any new promotional video, create a project-specific working copy of [VIDEO_CAMPAIGN_BRIEF](docs/templates/VIDEO_CAMPAIGN_BRIEF.md). Verify subject–stakes–clarity, align text/visual/voice, and show lock-in proof within the following 5–10 seconds. Lock an approved reference treatment and **test the opening scene and voice before the full render**; preserve approved scenes for targeted revisions. Complete the sound-on, mobile and factual QA checklist on the actual MP4 before marking it final.
- Before committing production effort to a new KDP niche, digital product or major software feature, complete [OPPORTUNITY_EVIDENCE_LOG](docs/templates/OPPORTUNITY_EVIDENCE_LOG.md) with actual problem and willingness-to-pay evidence. **New KDP proposals need 70/100 provisionally AND all mandatory evidence gates; an independent adversarial challenge must test the strongest reason to reject.** A passing score alone does not approve publication; prefer improving existing products when evidence is weak.
- Retain the approved Mercer Lane creative examples as references and improve their shortcomings; do not substitute generic AI defaults or add paid tools just because they appear in a YouTube demonstration.
- These templates are working documents. Do not commit unpublished manuscripts, personal customer information or raw copyrighted source media to this public repository.

## Execution and commercial review
- Diagnose the existing offer and funnel before creating more titles, tools or videos. For a proposed page change, record real buyer task, verified product contents, free-versus-paid fit, current checkout destination, the one behaviour being tested and a matched-period measurement plan. Do not imply that traffic, checkout clicks or impressions are paid sales.
- Use **research → production → independent QA → reviewable PR/preview → explicit approval → measurement** as a controlled sequence, not an unattended publishing agent. Keep one accountable reviewer for facts, arithmetic, privacy and platform compliance, separate from the production pass. Current ChatGPT/connected workflows may assist; never assume a particular subscription feature is available.
- Preserve approved output and keep changes minimal, with acceptance criteria and regression tests. Verify actual output rather than relying on an AI score or a guessed model benchmark.

## Non-negotiables
- Ground book descriptions, technical guidance, claims, imagery and CTAs in supplied verified source material. Do not invent cover imagery, bestseller status, prices, ratings, testimonials, results, endorsements or Amazon URLs.
- Match the site's actual current visual system in `src/styles/global.css`; do not fabricate fixed brand colours or change the public site's design merely because a creative reference uses them.
- Draft, fact-check, inspect on mobile and request owner approval before publishing new social content, books or product assets.
- Preserve the existing GitHub-first workflow: branch, build/test, pull request, preview, owner-approved merge. Never commit directly to main or merge on behalf of the owner without explicit instruction.
- Keep unpublished manuscripts, licensed media, credentials, API tokens and customer information out of this public repository.
- Marketing output must be useful in its own right and link to the genuine published book or product with a verified destination.
- For video: meaningful original vector animation rather than generic slideshow/icon movement; no whooshing/whizzing transition sounds; produce and inspect complete rendered output, not just scripts or stills.
- Differentiate impressions, visits, clicks and paid sales. Never infer conversions from views, link clicks or GA4 checkout-intent events.
- Prefer existing tools and low-cost workflows; evaluate licensing, provenance, security and actual cost before installing third-party software or AI models.
