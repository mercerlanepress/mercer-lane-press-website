Before creating more content, write down the business facts that should remain consistent everywhere they appear. Think of this as a **canonical business record** for internal use.

Useful fields include the business name, primary phone number, website, main contact route, address or service-area model, opening hours where relevant, principal services, locations genuinely served, and important booking or eligibility information.

## Why consistency matters

A changed phone number on the website, an old address in a directory, and two versions of the business name create avoidable ambiguity. The problem is not that every page must repeat identical wording. The problem is contradiction about facts that identify the entity.

## Audit primary sources first

Start with the places you control or officially manage:

- website contact and service pages;
- major business profiles;
- booking or contact pages;
- social profiles used as business references;
- important professional or trade profiles.

Correct the highest-authority source before trying to “outpublish” an error with new articles.

## Keep service and location claims precise

Do not expand a service area just to capture more searches. If travel, licensing, availability, or job type limits where the business works, state that clearly. Specific fit is more useful than broad but inaccurate coverage.

## Keep a change log

Record the date, source, old value, new value, and reason for meaningful corrections. When an AI answer continues to show an old fact, this gives you a practical trail to investigate rather than guessing which change mattered.

Once identity is stable, move to [service pages that explain fit clearly](/local-business-ai/service-pages-ai-visibility/).
