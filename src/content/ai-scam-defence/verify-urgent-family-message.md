An urgent voice note, text, or video is not proof of identity—even when it uses a familiar name, voice, or face. **Do not continue inside the incoming conversation. Pause, disconnect, and contact the person through a route you already trust.**

## Do this first

1. **Do not pay or disclose anything.** Never share a password, one-time security code, card number, or identity document in response to an unverified request.
2. **End the conversation.** Do not use a link, number, or account supplied by the requester.
3. **Call a saved number.** Use a number already in your contacts. If the person does not answer, contact another relative or trusted person who can check on them.
4. **Ask a useful question.** Confirm something relevant to the current situation. A prearranged [family safe-word plan](/ai-scam-defence/family-safe-word-plan/) can add another check, but it should not replace calling back independently.
5. **Verify organizations separately.** Open the official app yourself, use the number printed on a card, or type the organization's known website address. Do not trust caller ID by itself.
6. **Record what happened.** Save the message, username, number, time, and requested payment method without engaging further.

## Read the result correctly

- **The relative answers and is safe:** block and report the impersonating account. Warn other family members who might receive the same approach.
- **Nobody can reach the relative:** that uncertainty is not proof that the incoming request was real. Keep trying trusted contacts. If there is a credible immediate safety concern, contact local emergency services.
- **The requester resists verification:** pressure to stay on the line, keep a secret, or avoid another person is a warning sign. Stop engaging.
- **An organization says there is no issue:** use its official fraud-reporting route and protect any account details you revealed.

## Avoid a second mistake

Do not send a small “test” payment, provide a code to prove who you are, or install remote-access software. Do not confront the sender with personal facts they could reuse in a later attempt. Verification should move away from the requester's channel and back to people and services you chose independently.

If the approach arrived as a call, follow the [suspicious voice-call checklist](/ai-scam-defence/suspicious-voice-call/). If anything has already been sent or disclosed, move immediately to the [containment steps](/ai-scam-defence/after-sharing-money-or-information/).
