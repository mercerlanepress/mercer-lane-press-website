A transcript summary is not the same thing as a controlled meeting workflow.

Executive-support work is stronger when AI helps across the **meeting lifecycle** while a person preserves the difference between discussion, decision, action, ownership, and deadline.

## Before the meeting: build for decisions

Start with the meeting objective. What does the executive need to know, decide, ask, or avoid?

Combine the approved agenda with relevant background material and unresolved actions. AI can help condense threads, group related issues, and draft questions. It should not invent stakeholder positions or treat an old note as current fact.

A useful preparation brief can separate:

- purpose;
- current position;
- decisions required;
- open risks or questions; and
- logistics.

Keep the underlying sources visible so material points can be checked quickly.

## During the meeting: capture is not interpretation

If transcription or AI note-taking is permitted, treat the transcript as source material rather than finished minutes.

Names, numbers, technical terms, and speakers can be wrong. More importantly, the system may not know whether a suggestion became a decision, whether “we should” became a commitment, or whether an owner actually accepted an action.

Those are judgment questions.

## After the meeting: extract in layers

A useful first pass is to extract candidate:

- decisions;
- actions;
- owners;
- deadlines;
- unresolved questions; and
- parking-lot items.

Then verify each item against the authorized notes, transcript, or recording. Missing fields should stay visible. Use TO CONFIRM rather than assigning an owner or date because one seems likely.

Only after reconciliation should the verified register feed a follow-up email or tracker update.

## Keep an action register disciplined

An action needs an owner, an outcome, and a next review point. Vague entries are harder to close.

If a deadline was described only as “before the next steering meeting,” verify the actual meeting date in the live calendar instead of turning the phrase into a guessed date.

## Build follow-up from verified material

A polished recap can accidentally smooth disagreement into consensus or introduce new commitments for neatness.

Draft the follow-up from the verified decision-and-action register, keep unresolved points visible, and run the normal commitment and disclosure check before release.
