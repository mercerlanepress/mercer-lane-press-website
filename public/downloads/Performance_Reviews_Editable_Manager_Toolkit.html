Completed fictional review example

The employee, workplace, numbers and outcomes in this case are invented and do not establish a standard for any real employer.

Preparation and evidence

ScopeJordan Ellis, Customer Operations Specialist, Northstar Services; January–December
Quality37 of 40 eligible handoffs passed first check; three corrected before release.
Timeliness45 of 48 eligible weekly updates on time; two of the three delays depended on supplier approval, one lacked an advance risk flag.
Communication28 of 30 sampled replies included the required next step; two corrected after coaching.
StrengthSix sampled cross-team escalations had clear owners and next steps.
Rating statusProvisional pending employer rating anchors and authorized review.

Review narrative

Jordan’s handoffs generally met the organization’s accuracy requirement: 37 of 40 sampled eligible handoffs passed the required first check. Three source-reference corrections were completed before release. Forty-five of 48 eligible weekly status updates met the agreed Friday deadline. Two later updates involved supplier approvals; one did not contain the agreed advance risk flag. The next cycle will use a Thursday dependency review and a source-check refresher. The record does not establish a general defect in Jordan’s character or commitment.

Conversation and goal

Jordan raised an earlier notification about supplier timing. The manager agreed to verify it before finalizing that portion, supply a Thursday reminder and example status format, and review the next six weeks. The next ten eligible handoffs would use an approved source checklist with a short refresher; six weekly cycles would test timely risk flags and approved exceptions. A voluntary peer demonstration would be offered within working time.

Follow-up and manager self-audit

Ten of ten eligible handoffs passed the first check. Six weekly updates met the deadline or documented exception method. The manager supplied the agreed support and clarified the status-risk channel prospectively. This shows short-term improvement, not proof that a long-term issue has been solved. Final records belong in the authorized employer system.
